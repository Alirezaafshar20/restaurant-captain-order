# Menu sources

Menu, prices and food descriptions were read from https://moyacuisine.com/menu-home/moya/فارسی on 2026-09-05 for a private MOYA presentation. `lib/menu.ts` contains selected items, not a claim that the complete official menu has been imported.

Food photos in `public/menu` retain original names. Their source URLs are `https://moyacuisine.com/api/images/` followed by the filename; mappings to dish IDs are in `lib/image-map.ts`. They are actual official-menu photos, not generated depictions. An absent image uses a neutral menu icon, never a different dish's photograph.

Font source: https://moyacuisine.com/fonts/AbarHigh-Regular.woff2. Rights remain with the respective owners; restaurant approval for public brand/asset use is part of rollout.
